Research

The problems I work on, and where each one has taken me.

MRI · Generative models

Contrast-free DCE-MRI synthesis

Synthesising early and late dynamic contrast-enhanced prostate MRI from routine non-contrast sequences, using GAN, transformer and residual diffusion models.

5 papers · MIDL 2026, CVPR-W 2024

Digital pathology · WSI

Brain histopathology analysis

Tile-level classification and spatial segmentation of whole-slide images; benchmarking pathology foundation models (UNI, GigaPath) and fusing their embeddings.

Ongoing · SGBC, IIT Madras

CT · MRA · Segmentation

3D anatomical segmentation

Vertebral instance segmentation from CT for pedicle-screw planning (14 vertebrae, VerSe 2019), and cerebral vessel segmentation from MRA for stroke CFD studies.

Deployed as Docker microservices

Restoration · Translation
